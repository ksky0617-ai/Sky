## Project Vision

Olibana is a Tokyo-based luxury design atelier. Its mission is to craft world-class products whose beauty and identity spring *only* from nature's architecture.

We do **not** mimic cultural symbols or logos. We use nature's engineering as our design vocabulary. Over years and collections, the aim is for Olibana to be recognizable by style alone.

## Core Values

- **Nature as Structure** — Nature provides the rules of form and function.
- **Timeless Quality** — Designs that gain depth over time rather than date.
- **Pure Materials** — Natural fibers and minerals chosen for longevity and evolving patina.
- **Minimalism** — Removing noise to reveal essence.
- **Craftsmanship** — Every piece is meticulously made; machines may assist, but handcraft is essential.
- **Silence & Refinement** — Elegance through understatement and calm presence.

## Brand Promise

Every Olibana object — garment or accessory — embodies a design rule inspired by nature. It should feel inevitable, as if it *emerged* from the landscape or the flow of water rather than from human whimsy.

When people see an Olibana design, even without a logo, they should feel its quiet sophistication and connect with the natural principle behind it.

## Brand Identity Keywords

- Quietly Elegant
- Intelligent Precision
- Confident Simplicity
- Inherently Organic
- Emotionally Calming

## Applying the Brand Bible

Use this document to check *intent* — whether a piece, a campaign, or a line of copy is saying what Olibana says. Use [`Character_Bible.md`](./Character_Bible.md) to check *voice*, and [`Design_System.md`](./Design_System.md) to check *form*.

A design that satisfies the Design System but violates a Core Value does not ship.
