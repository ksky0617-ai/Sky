# River Atlas

**Spec Version:** v1.0
**Domain:** Water — flow, curvature, reflection
**Extracts:** Rhythmic proportions, sinuous line systems, flow-inspired silhouettes

---

## Focus of Study

- **Flow & Turbulence** — Observe how water speed varies in straight vs. curved sections.
- **Curve Radius** — Note the range of curve radii in river bends (meanders).
- **River Width & Depth** — Measure how channel width changes, and how depth profiles affect appearance.
- **Surface Reflection** — Study how light reflects off moving water at different angles.
- **Stone Configuration** — Examine how boulders and riverbed rocks interrupt flow, creating eddies and patterns.

## Measurement Templates

| Parameter | Unit | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow Velocity | m/s | Flowmeter, or time a floating object over a known distance |
| Curvature | m | Radius of prominent river bends |
| Channel Width | m | Measure across the water surface at multiple points |
| Depth Profile | m | Chart depth at fixed intervals across the channel |
| Reflection Angle | ° | Angle of sunlight vs. observer at key times (governs perceived sparkle) |
| Stone Size & Spacing | cm | Catalog common rock diameters and spacing intervals |

## Observations

- **Line Rhythm** — Flowing water creates parallel ripples and wave fronts on the surface; these suggest fluid line patterns.
- **Flow Path** — The "S" shapes of water passing obstacles inspire smooth, continuous contours.
- **Contrast** — Foam lines against clear water are a cue for light/dark contrast in textures.
- **Elasticity** — Water both wraps around and bounces off rocks; this interplay informs how fabric moves and falls.
- **Patterns** — Repeating ripple motifs on sandbanks translate into prints and embroidery repetition.

## Design Translation

From these studies, designers extract a *rhythm* and *proportion system* for silhouette flow:

- A long coat hem mirrors a gentle river meander.
- Pleat spacing echoes the spacing of parallel streamlines around rocks.
- Drape resistance is tuned to observed eddy behavior — where fabric wraps and where it releases.
