## Persona

Olibana's persona is **elegant, quiet, intelligent, and confident** — a composed, mindful individual who leads by example rather than by boasting.

- **Elegant (Refined)** — Every detail is considered. No superfluous flair, only disciplined beauty.
- **Quiet (Reserved)** — Speaks softly and purposefully. Prefers listening and observation over loud statements.
- **Intelligent (Thoughtful)** — Designs are intellectually grounded. The brand *knows* the mathematics and mechanics behind a form.
- **Confident** — Sure of its aesthetic choices. Does not chase validation or trends.

**Never aggressive or showy.** Olibana's style does not flaunt or compete. It offers a serene confidence that stands out precisely because it does not demand attention.

## Voice in Practice

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| State the natural rule behind a form | Use superlatives about the brand itself |
| Let a single fact carry the sentence | Stack adjectives for emphasis |
| Describe what a piece *is* | Describe what the wearer will *become* |
| Leave silence where nothing needs saying | Fill space with atmosphere copy |
| Name the measurement | Gesture vaguely at "nature" |

## Visual Expression

Where branding is present, Olibana uses:

- **Muted palettes** — derived from [`Light_Atlas.md`](./Light_Atlas.md) observations, never arbitrary.
- **Minimal embellishment** — no ornament that does not carry structural meaning.
- **Typography with clear geometry** — reflecting the calm, rational spirit of the brand.

Restraint is the through-line: the persona is legible in what is *left out* as much as in what is shown.

## 4. Photography Direction

*Added v1.1. Governs the eleven image types named in the website directive (§47).*

The persona traits map directly to camera decisions. A photograph is Olibana when a viewer could infer the four traits from the frame alone.

| Trait | Camera consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Elegant** | One subject per frame. Composition resolves; nothing is cropped by accident. |
| **Quiet** | Available light preferred. No hard key light, no rim-light drama, no lens flare. |
| **Intelligent** | The structural logic of the garment is visible — seam, drape, panel, edge. |
| **Confident** | The subject does not perform for the camera. No appeal to the viewer. |

### Standing rules

- **Light** — Natural or naturalistic. Direction is legible; the viewer can tell where the light comes from. Sourced from a `Light_Atlas.md` observation, not invented.
- **Shadow** — Present, never crushed. Shadow carries structural information and must retain detail.
- **Background** — Never a styled set dressed to look natural. Either a real location or an honest neutral ground.
- **Colour** — Grade toward the measured atmosphere of the recorded hour. Never toward a "look".
- **Grain** — Permitted where it is a property of the capture. Never added as texture styling.
- **Motion blur** — Permitted where it records real movement of cloth or body. Never simulated.
- **Crop** — Cuts fall on structural lines, not arbitrarily. An edge in the frame corresponds to an edge in the garment.

### Model direction

- Posture is upright and unforced. No leaning into the lens, no motion held for effect.
- Gaze is away from the lens by default. Direct gaze is permitted once per editorial sequence at most.
- Movement is walking, turning, standing, sitting — real actions at real speed.
- **Never:** appeal, seduction, aspiration signalling, luxury-lifestyle staging.

### Absolute exclusions

Restating the directive's prohibitions as photographic rules: no cherry blossom, no Mount Fuji, no torii, no kimono reference, no calligraphy or kanji used decoratively, no zen-garden staging, no black-and-gold luxury grade, no beige-minimalism styling. Olibana photographs *Japanese nature's structure*, never *Japanese cultural iconography*.

## 5. Web Voice

*Added v1.1. Governs interface copy — the states the brand speaks in most often.*

The persona is hardest to hold in failure states, where most brands either apologise excessively or turn playful. Olibana does neither. **In an interface, "quiet" means the copy states the situation and the next action, and stops.**

| State | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Loading** | Name what is arriving, or say nothing. Never entertain. | `Loading` — or silence |
| **Empty** | State the emptiness plainly, then offer one route out. | `Nothing saved yet.` + one link |
| **Error** | What happened → what you can do → action. No apology theatre. | `This didn't send. Check the address and try again.` |
| **404** | No wordplay. No "lost in the forest" metaphor. | `This page doesn't exist.` + recovery paths |
| **Sold out** | Direct, without urgency pressure. | `Sold out.` Never `Hurry — almost gone!` |
| **Confirmation** | Facts before feeling. The order number precedes the thank-you. | `Order confirmed.` then details, then the brand line |
| **Form validation** | Say what is needed, not what was done wrong. | `Enter a delivery address` not `Invalid input!` |

### Standing rules

- No exclamation marks anywhere in interface copy.
- No emoji.
- Sentence case. Never all-caps for emphasis (all-caps is permitted only as a typographic device in navigation and section labels).
- No urgency or scarcity manipulation — no countdowns, no "only 2 left", no "X people viewing".
- No first-person plural warmth performance (`We're so excited to...`).
- One idea per sentence. If a sentence needs a comma splice, it is two sentences.
- Never apologise more than once, and never for something that is not the brand's fault.

## 6. Motion Character

*Added v1.1. The persona expressed as movement — see `docs/website/04_MOTION_LANGUAGE.md` for token values.*

| Trait | Motion consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Elegant** | Movement resolves fully. Nothing snaps, nothing overshoots. |
| **Quiet** | One thing moves at a time. Motion never competes for attention with content. |
| **Intelligent** | Every movement carries meaning — it shows relationship, hierarchy, or origin. |
| **Confident** | Motion is unhurried. The interface does not rush to prove responsiveness. |

**Olibana does not bounce.** Overshoot is the motion equivalent of an exclamation mark.
