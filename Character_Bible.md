## Persona

Olibana's persona is **elegant, quiet, intelligent, and confident** — a composed, mindful individual who leads by example rather than by boasting.

- **Elegant (Refined)** — Every detail is considered. No superfluous flair, only disciplined beauty.
- **Quiet (Reserved)** — Speaks softly and purposefully. Prefers listening and observation over loud statements.
- **Intelligent (Thoughtful)** — Designs are intellectually grounded. The brand *knows* the mathematics and mechanics behind a form.
- **Confident** — Sure of its aesthetic choices. Does not chase validation or trends.

**Never aggressive or showy.** Olibana's style does not flaunt or compete. It offers a serene confidence that stands out precisely because it does not demand attention.

## Voice in Practice

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| State the natural rule behind a form | Use superlatives about the brand itself |
| Let a single fact carry the sentence | Stack adjectives for emphasis |
| Describe what a piece *is* | Describe what the wearer will *become* |
| Leave silence where nothing needs saying | Fill space with atmosphere copy |
| Name the measurement | Gesture vaguely at "nature" |

## Visual Expression

Where branding is present, Olibana uses:

- **Muted palettes** — derived from [`Light_Atlas.md`](./Light_Atlas.md) observations, never arbitrary.
- **Minimal embellishment** — no ornament that does not carry structural meaning.
- **Typography with clear geometry** — reflecting the calm, rational spirit of the brand.

Restraint is the through-line: the persona is legible in what is *left out* as much as in what is shown.
